About MK Thunder Stats Centre

2026/27 Season

About the site

MK Thunder Stats Centre is an independent fan-created statistics site following Milton Keynes Thunder throughout the 2026/27 season.

The aim is simple: to bring fixtures, results, player statistics, netminder statistics and league and cup tables together in one easy-to-use place for Thunder supporters.

What you'll find here

Fixtures & Results
Keep track of upcoming MK Thunder games and completed results throughout the season.
Player Stats
Follow goals, assists, points, penalty minutes and other player statistics.
Netminder Stats
Track games played, shots faced, saves, save percentage, shutouts and results.
League Table
Follow MK Thunder's position throughout the NIHL season.
Cup Table
Follow Thunder's progress through the Eddie Joseph Memorial Cup group.
Thunder Roster
View the current MK Thunder squad, player numbers and positions.

Independent fan site

MK Thunder Stats Centre is an independent fan project and is not an official Milton Keynes Thunder, NIHL or England Ice Hockey website.

Every effort is made to keep the information and statistics accurate and up to date, but occasional errors may occur.
